01

Overview

Welcome to Aurelia Stays. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

02

Information We Collect

When you use Aurelia Stays, we may collect information that helps us provide and improve our services.

Personal Information

Name, email address, phone number and account information that you provide when creating an account.

Booking Information

Details about your reservations, selected hotels, travel dates and booking preferences.

Device Information

Basic technical information such as browser type, device type and interaction with our website.

03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide a smooth and reliable booking experience.

  • Process and manage hotel reservations.
  • Send booking confirmations and updates.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve website performance and services.
  • Detect suspicious or unauthorized activity.
  • Send relevant offers when you have chosen to receive marketing communications.
04

Information Sharing

Aurelia Stays does not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our services.

We only share information when necessary.

For example, information may be shared with a hotel or accommodation provider to complete your reservation.

05

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Aurelia Stays may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand website usage and improve your experience.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

06

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Secure Access

Account information is protected using appropriate security controls.

Protected Data

We take reasonable precautions when handling personal information.
